What does "last used" mean, and what if I delete one?

Last used is the last time a password opened a backup; Sanctum tries the most recently used one first.

Backups already checked stay as they are. But if one of them needs to be re-checked later, Sanctum will need a saved password that opens it. Keep old passwords until nothing depends on them.
