Create your vault

Choose a passphrase. It protects everything Sanctum stores.

There is no recovery. If this passphrase is lost, the vault can never be opened again. Save it in your password manager first, then paste it into both boxes.
Both entries must match exactly. Nothing is created if they differ.
Why a passphrase, and why so strict?

Your backups are encrypted, and Sanctum keeps the passwords to open them in a sealed index. That index is locked with this passphrase. Because the passphrase is never stored anywhere, nobody who gets hold of the server's disks can read your data. The trade-off is that you must type it after every restart.

Unlock your vault

The vault is locked. Enter the passphrase to open it.

Why do I have to unlock after every restart?

The passphrase lives only in memory and is never written to disk. That keeps your backups unreadable to anyone who copies the server's drives. The cost is that a restart always locks the vault until you unlock it.

Too many wrong tries in a row are slowed down automatically. A typo will not lock you out for long.

If the passphrase is forgotten, the vault cannot be recovered. Keep a copy in your password manager.
